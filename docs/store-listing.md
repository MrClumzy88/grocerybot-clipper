# Chrome Web Store listing

Text for the store dashboard's **Store listing** tab. Keep the disclaimer at
the end of the description in sync with the README's Disclaimer section.

## Description

CouponSweep clips every digital coupon on your store account in one click, so
you don't have to press "Clip" hundreds of times.

Works with Kroger, H-E-B, Target Circle, Walmart Cash, CVS ExtraCare,
Walgreens, BJ's, Safeway, Albertsons, Tom Thumb and about 30 more grocery and
pharmacy chains (Ralphs, Fry's, King Soopers, Fred Meyer, Vons, Jewel-Osco,
Acme, Shaw's and others).

How it works
• Sign in to your store's website as you normally would.
• Check the stores you shop at in the CouponSweep popup.
• Click Clip, or let it run once a day.

It clips one coupon at a time with short pauses, and stops with a plain
message if the store asks you to sign in or complete a check. Where a card
holds a limited number of coupons (Kroger, Walgreens), the biggest savings are
clipped first.

Private: no account, no servers, no analytics. CouponSweep never sees your
password and sends nothing anywhere except to the store itself.

Free and open source: https://github.com/MrClumzy88/couponsweep

DISCLAIMER: CouponSweep is an unofficial tool. It isn't affiliated with or
endorsed by any of the stores named above; their names are used only to say
which sites it works with. Automatically clipping coupons may go against a
store's terms of use, and a store could limit, suspend or close an account it
believes is automated. Use it only for personal, low-volume use on your own
account, at your own risk.

## Single purpose (Privacy tab)

Clips available digital coupons on supported grocery and pharmacy store
websites the user is already signed in to.
